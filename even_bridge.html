How to Build a Flat Bridge:


What You Need:

    Campfire (2 campfires per bridge block)
    Spruce Slabs (4 per bridge block)
    Spruce Fence Gates (2 per bridge block)
    8x Spruce Stairs (4 at each end)
    8x Cobblestone Wall (4 at each end)

Decoration (optional but recommended):

    Lanterns (2 per bridge section)
    Cobblestone Wall (2 per bridge section)

Construction:


    If you find a slope, river, or similar terrain, start with the first end of the bridge.
    Build this first "ring" as follows:

    Once you've built this ring, create a similar ring, but replace the stairs with slabs.

    Repeat this step as many times as needed until you reach the other side. Also, leave an additional block of space. The other end will be built there later.

    IMPORTANT: If you include decoration, you need to add an extra step at the middle of the bridge:

    This step can be added approximately every 7-10 blocks on a long bridge.
    Finally, build the special ring at the other end of the bridge, same as the beginning, and you're done.

Additional Option:

You can also build an intersection of two bridges. For example: an intersection of two bridges crossing a valley.
